### Notes

- WSL2 reports fewer physical cores than native Windows on the same box (10 vs 14); treat cross-OS ratios on this host as directional, not absolute.
- DrvFS (`/mnt/c`) adds I/O overhead during restore/build; benchmark **Mean** columns are still dominated by in-memory hot paths once the process is running.
